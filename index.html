Why This Happens

Nobody is responsible for the whole picture.

Your CPA optimizes the tax return. Your attorney drafts the documents. Your agent writes the policies. Your advisor manages the portfolio. Every one of them is good at their piece — but not one of them is checking whether the pieces fit together.

Nobody is ever tasked with the follow‑through. Whether the retirement account is actually funded. Whether the operating agreement and the will name the same business successor. Whether the beneficiaries you named in 2011 are still the ones you’d name today.

Families are rarely hurt by bad advice — just good advice pushed to tomorrow.

It still takes time and effort to act on good advice. The pieces you’ve been handed still have to be fitted together. But nobody has ever shown you how, or what the finished picture is supposed to look like — which is exactly why it’s so hard to tell how close you are.

Right now, the follow‑through falls to whichever of you can give something else up — and even then, it usually waits until something forces the issue.

The Work

What I actually do.

A combination of paperwork, strategy, and clarification.

01

Succession & Continuity

Who runs it, who owns it, and who continues the legacy — not assumed, but in writing. I map it with you, then work with your attorney to document and fund it — and to make sure it agrees with everything else you’ve signed.

02

Business Strategy & Owner Decisions

A strategic partner for the decisions that don’t have an obvious owner: modeling a new business line, weighing buy or lease on the building, evaluating a growth opportunity. All of it to build value you could transfer.

03

Legacy & What Gets Passed On

What the business is for, what you want to pass on to your children, what you want to protect them from, and what you want your family to value in thirty years. The conversation most plans skip.

04

Advisor Coordination & Translation

One point of contact for your CPA, attorney, insurance professional, controller, banker, and financial advisor. I organize what each of them is asking for, translate their jargon into a decision you can make, and fill the gaps between them.

05

Documentation & Entity Organization

Gathering, organizing, and maintaining the paperwork behind your business and household: entity documents, records, policies, and access information — all in one place, current, and easy for either of you to find.

06

Household Budgeting & Gap Analysis

Personal cash flow, income statement, balance sheet, and general retirement projections, along with a standing, prioritized list of what’s open, who it belongs with, and when it needs to happen. The Readiness Inventory on this page is a short version of that list — the real one is longer and specific to your family.

Your Existing Team

Good. I don’t replace them.
