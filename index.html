What I Do

One point of contact.
Every moving part.

Running a business and running a household both generate paperwork, deadlines, and a rotating cast of professionals — a CPA, an attorney, a financial advisor, a bookkeeper — who rarely talk to each other. Usually that coordination falls on one person by default, not by choice.

I take that on. I organize the documents, translate what each advisor is asking for, flag the gaps in what’s already in place, and keep the whole picture moving — so it isn’t sitting on just one of you.

Advisor Coordination & Translation

I work alongside your existing CPA, attorney, and financial advisor — organizing communication, clarifying what each one is asking for, and making sure nothing falls between them.

Documentation & Entity Organization

Gathering, organizing, and maintaining the entity documents, records, and paperwork behind the business and the household — and flagging what’s missing.

Budgeting & Gap Analysis

Household budgeting and general retirement projections, plus a clear-eyed look at where your current plans have gaps — so you know what to raise with your advisors, and when.
